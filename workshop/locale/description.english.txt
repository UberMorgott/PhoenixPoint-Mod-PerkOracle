[b]Oracle is an in-game advisor and wiki for Phoenix Point. It shows hidden information where decisions are made and, if enabled, lets you change rolled personal perks.[/b]

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Information and previews[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Rolled-perk foresight:[/b] highlights randomly rolled personal perk slots and shows the full pool each slot can roll from.
[*][b]Class wiki:[/b] click a class skill to view the class's complete perk layout, including classes added by other mods.
[*][b]Event outcomes:[/b] preview what each event choice grants, with rewards scaled to your difficulty settings.
[*][b]Subclass selection:[/b] preview a subclass's perks and abilities before committing.
[*][b]Dismantle yield:[/b] item tooltips show the actual resources returned when scrapped.
[/list]

[h1]Perk swapping[/h1]
[i]Optional and off by default. Oracle does not change perks unless you enable this feature and confirm a swap.[/i]
[list]
[*]Right-click a rolled personal perk slot to choose a replacement.
[*]Every swap uses the game's confirmation dialog and shows the exact skill-point price before charging it.
[*]Revert a slot to its original perk at any time; the original is recorded per campaign in the savegame and travels with the save.
[/list]

[h1]TFTV drills[/h1]
When Terror From The Void is installed, the class wiki also covers every drill.
[list]
[*]View each drill's class, minimum level, required research, and weapon proficiency.
[*]See whether a drill is available, already taken, or locked, including the reason it is locked.
[*]Swap drills into personal perk slots through the same priced confirmation process.
[/list]
Two TFTV-only options are available and [b]off by default[/b]: ignore drill unlock requirements, and allow re-swapping drills already owned.

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
[list]
[*]Works standalone with Phoenix Point or alongside Terror From The Void.
[*]TFTV-specific drill support and prorated dismantle refunds activate only when TFTV is present.
[*]Works with mods that add classes.
[/list]
