[h1]PerkOracle[/h1]
[b]A read-only in-game preview of which "personal" perks a soldier could have rolled — for Phoenix Point (TFTV). Optional, off-by-default perk swapping for players who want more control.[/b]

[b]PerkOracle does not generate, add, or change any perks.[/b] In Phoenix Point, some "personal"-track perks are rolled randomly per soldier — that randomness is the [b]game's own system[/b] (vanilla and TFTV), not something this mod adds. PerkOracle is a UI / quality-of-life mod that only [b]reads[/b] the data the game already has, and shows it to you clearly.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]What it does[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Highlights rolled perks[/b] — on the ability-progression screen, the personal-track cells whose perk was randomly rolled get a distinct blue tint, so you can tell at a glance which perks came from the random roll versus the fixed class track.
[*][b]Candidate "wiki" popup[/b] — over a rolled cell, press [b]cancel[/b] (right-click or [b]Esc[/b]) to open a popup listing [i]every[/i] perk that could have rolled into that slot. Each candidate is shown with the game's [b]native icon and native name/description tooltip[/b]. The popup is a temporary window — it is [b]not[/b] a new progression row, and nothing is changed by opening it. Cancel again to close.
[/list]

PerkOracle does [b]not[/b] alter perk generation, does [b]not[/b] add perks or progression rows, and does [b]not[/b] modify the ability track. With swapping off (the default) it is a pure, view-only preview.

[h1]Optional: Perk Swap (off by default)[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]AllowPerkSwap[/b] — default [b]OFF[/b]. When you enable it in the config, [b]left-clicking[/b] a perk in the wiki replaces an [b]already-learned[/b] perk in that slot with another existing perk — for players who want more control over their soldiers.
[*]This creates [b]no new perks[/b]; it only swaps which existing perk occupies a slot you have already unlocked. Leaving it off keeps PerkOracle a strictly read-only preview tool.
[/list]

[h1]Requirements[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Phoenix Point[/b]
[*][b]Terror From The Void (TFTV)[/b] overhaul — [i]phoenixrising.tftv[/i]
[/list]

English, 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese), Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Polski, Русский, Español — 8 languages.

[h1]How to use[/h1]
[list=1]
[*]Install and enable the [b]TFTV[/b] overhaul first.
[*][b]Subscribe[/b] to PerkOracle here on the Workshop.
[*]Launch Phoenix Point and [b]enable PerkOracle[/b] in the in-game mod manager (it loads after TFTV).
[*]Open a soldier's ability-progression screen, find a blue-tinted (rolled) cell, and press [b]cancel[/b] (right-click / Esc) to open the candidate wiki.
[/list]
