[h1]PerkOracle[/h1]
[b]An in-game wiki of rollable perks for Phoenix Point (TFTV) — with optional perk swapping.[/b]

Ever wondered which random "personal" perks a soldier could have rolled into a slot? PerkOracle shows you, right on the ability-progression screen, as a clean in-game popup that reuses the game's own ability tooltips.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]What it does[/h2]
Under [b]Terror From The Void (TFTV)[/b], some personal perks are randomly rolled per soldier. PerkOracle reads TFTV's own configuration to know exactly which perks each slot can produce, so the preview is always accurate.

[list]
[*][b]Rolled-perk highlight[/b] — on the ability-progression grid, cells whose perk was randomly rolled get a distinct highlighted background, so rolled perks stand out from fixed and class perks at a glance.
[*][b]Candidate wiki popup[/b] — hover a rolled cell and press [b]cancel[/b] (right-click or [b]Esc[/b]) to open a popup listing [i]every[/i] perk that could roll into that slot. Candidates use the game's native ability cell and the native framed name/description tooltip on hover. Cancel again to close.
[*][b]Optional perk swap[/b] — when enabled in the settings, [b]left-click[/b] any perk in the wiki to replace the soldier's already-learned perk in that slot. Free and reversible — for players who prefer an easier game.
[*][b]Non-destructive by default[/b] — fixed and class cells are never changed; with swapping off, the wiki is a pure, view-only preview.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Phoenix Point[/b]
[*][b]Terror From The Void (TFTV)[/b] overhaul — [i]phoenixrising.tftv[/i]
[/list]

[h2]Supported languages[/h2]
English, 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese), Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Polski, Русский, Español — 8 languages.

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[list=1]
[*]Install and enable the [b]TFTV[/b] overhaul first.
[*][b]Subscribe[/b] to PerkOracle here on the Workshop.
[*]Launch Phoenix Point and [b]enable PerkOracle[/b] in the in-game mod manager (it loads after TFTV).
[*]Open a soldier's ability-progression screen, hover a highlighted (rolled) cell, and press [b]cancel[/b] (right-click / Esc) to open the wiki.
[/list]

[h2]Configuration[/h2]
In the in-game mod settings:
[list]
[*][b]AllowPerkSwap[/b] — default [b]OFF[/b]. When off, the wiki is a pure preview (view-only). When on, left-clicking a perk in the wiki replaces the soldier's learned perk in that slot (free).
[/list]
